Mail, calendar and contacts.
All in one place.

Almanac is a fast, friendly desktop app for everything in your inbox and on your schedule. It works with Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail and any IMAP server.

Download Almanac View on GitHub
For Linux, Windows and macOS

Everything you need, nothing in the way

Almanac connects straight to your accounts. It's quick with huge mailboxes and pleasant to write in.

All your accounts

Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, AOL, iCloud, Fastmail, Proton Bridge, Zoho and more, or any IMAP account.

Fast with huge mailboxes

Hundreds of thousands of messages scroll smoothly. Search is instant, and your mail is there offline.

Private by design

No middleman service. Almanac talks to your servers directly and keeps your passwords in the system keychain.

Lovely to write in

Rich text, tables, pasted Markdown, and code blocks with syntax highlighting. Spelling and grammar are checked offline.

A real calendar

Day, week, month and year views. Answer invitations from the message, and join Zoom in one click.

Organized your way

Conversations, VIPs, coloured flags, smart mailboxes, reminders, mute, block, and trackpad swipes.

Your week at a glance

Calendars from Google, iCloud, Fastmail or any CalDAV server, side by side in your own colours. A mini month sits in the corner.

Meeting links turn into a Join button, and event details show everything at once.

Write like you think

Paste Markdown and it becomes formatting. Type three backticks for a code block, highlighted in its language.

Tables, lists and signatures are built in, and replies can be written right under the message.

Sign in, not set up

Pick your provider and sign in. Almanac finds the right servers for you.

For Google, a guided setup does the fiddly parts in your own browser.

Take a look

Click any picture to see it full size. These use made-up sample data, which you can try too: choose Try with Sample Data on the welcome screen.

Download

Get the files from the latest release on GitHub.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

  • Installer: …-windows-x64-setup.exe
  • Portable: …-windows-x64-portable.zip. Unzip it and run Almanac.exe.

macOS

macOS 13 Ventura or later. With Homebrew:

brew install --cask benwbooth/tap/almanac

Or the disk image: …-macos-arm64.dmg (Apple Silicon) or …-macos-x86_64.dmg (Intel).

Linux

Flatpak, with updates:

flatpak install --user https://benwbooth.github.io/almanac/flatpak/almanac.flatpakref

Or the …-x86_64.AppImage: make it executable and run it. Nix users can run nix run github:benwbooth/almanac.

The macOS app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Homebrew handles that for you. From the disk image, right-click Almanac and choose Open the first time.
