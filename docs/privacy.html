Privacy policy

Updated 16 November 2026

Almanac is a desktop app for your mail, calendars and contacts. It runs on your own computer. There is no Almanac server, no account with us, and no analytics or tracking.

What Almanac reads

When you sign in with Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, AOL or another mail provider, Almanac connects straight from your computer to that provider to download and send your mail, and to read and change your calendars and contacts. If you allow Google Drive, Dropbox or Box, Almanac uploads only the attachments you choose to send as links.

Where it is kept

Your mail, calendars and contacts are stored only on your computer, in Almanac's data folder. Sign-in tokens and passwords are kept in your system's keychain. Nothing is sent to the author of Almanac or to anyone other than the providers you sign in to.

Sharing

Almanac does not sell, share or transfer your data. Almanac's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Remove an account in Almanac's Accounts window to delete its local copy, or delete Almanac's data folder. You can revoke Almanac's access at any time from your provider's account security page (for Google, myaccount.google.com/permissions).
